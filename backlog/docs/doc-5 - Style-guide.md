# Style guide

Every asset meets this guide, whether drawn by hand or generated by a recipe. It turns the proposal in "Visual language and asset generation" and decision D21 into rules a script can check.

The palette lives in code: `packages/core/src/palette.ts` exports `OUTLINE`, `RAMPS`, `PALETTE`, and `BIOME_RAMPS`. Change colours there and nowhere else. The art gallery shows the rules at `/art.html?section=style`: every ramp, each biome's ramp set, and pack sprites on the 16px grid.

## Palette

- The master palette has 64 colours: the outline plus 18 named ramps.
- It keeps all 53 colours of Ninja Adventure's `Palette.png`.
- It adds seven colours the recipe spike needed. The pack's own sheets already use some of them: `#23403C`, `#2A4B3F`, `#3F6E4C`, `#4A7F4B`, `#7FA24A`, `#D5D66B`, `#7B473C`.
- It adds four colours for the new biomes: `#6B6A2C` (dark straw), `#7C88B8` and `#A4B8DA` (snow shadows), and `#D2C9C9` (pale granite, from the pack's floor sheet).
- Every pixel of a shipped or generated sprite is `OUTLINE` or a ramp colour. Nothing else ships.

## Ramps

- A ramp is one material's colours, ordered dark to light, 4 to 6 steps.
- Shade a surface by stepping along its ramp. Do not jump to another ramp to darken or lighten.
- Ramps hue-shift. Shadows lean cool (teal, violet, blue). Highlights lean warm (yellow, cream).
- Neighbouring ramps share colours where materials meet. `grass` and `pine` share `#345A52`; `bark` and `sand` share `#7B473C`.

## Biomes

Each biome draws its ground and its flora (plants and rocks) from a named set of ramps. `BIOME_RAMPS` holds the sets.

| Biome | Ground | Flora |
| --- | --- | --- |
| meadow | grass, soil | grass, bark, poppy, water, gold |
| forest | pine, soil | grass, pine, bark, stone, peach |
| lakeland | grass, sand, water | grass, straw, bark, rose |
| scrubland | straw, soil | sage, pine, straw, bark, stone |
| desert | dune, sand | cactus, sage, straw, sand |
| highlands | grass, soil, granite | pine, bark, heather, gold, granite |
| taiga | snow, pine | pine, bark, gold, poppy |
| tundra | snow, stone | sage, snow, stone |

## Light

- Light comes from the top left. Top and left faces take the light ramp steps; bottom and right faces take the dark steps.
- Draw the way the pack draws, not with physical shading. Smooth shaded blobs read as a different art style.
- Canopies: a flat top, slanted light bands, and lighter drips over each band edge. Vary drip spacing.
- Rocks: a lit top plane over a darker front face. Move cracks between variants.
- Cacti: columns in fixed steps. Vary widths and arms.

## Outline

- Every object gets a full 1px outline in `#141B1B`.
- Ground gets no outline.
- Do not lighten the outline on the lit side. The pack never does, and lightened sprites stand out.

## Scale and anchoring

- Tiles are 16px. The view is three-quarter top-down.
- An object anchors bottom-centre on its tile. Taller sprites overhang the tiles above.
- Trees are one to three tiles tall. Bushes, rocks, and flowers fit one tile.
- Characters use the pack's 16px walk sheets.

## Detail density

- A ground tile uses at most 3 colours.
- Ground stays low in contrast, so objects and players read against it.

## Per-sprite limits

- An object sprite uses at most 8 colours, outline included.
- Pixels are fully opaque or fully transparent. Shadows are the one exception: a palette colour at partial alpha.
- No stray pixels. Every opaque pixel touches another opaque pixel on one of its four sides.

## Recipes and hand-drawn art

- Trees, conifers, cacti, and rocks come from code recipes (D21).
- Bushes, flowers, and other small props start from a hand-drawn base. Recipes recolour and vary it, or it ships as drawn.
- Both follow this guide, and the palette lint checks both.
