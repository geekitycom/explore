## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
The world is one persistent map that every player shares, but today nobody can change it. Let players leave lasting, deliberate traces that others find later, even when they are never online at the same time: names on the landmarks they discover, rocks moved and stacked into cairns, and flowers carried to graves. Traces are scarce because the world makes them so, not because of daily allowances: each landmark can be named once, rocks are only ever moved and never created, and flowers must be picked and carried. Traces sit on top of the generated terrain and never change stored generator output (decision D23).
