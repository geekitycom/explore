## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
The world is one persistent map that every player shares, but today nobody can change it. Let players leave lasting, deliberate traces that others find later, even when they are never online at the same time: signs with short notes, cairns, and flowers at graves. Leaving a trace is scarce on purpose. Each player has a small allowance that refills slowly, so what they leave is chosen with care and the world does not fill with clutter. Traces sit on top of the generated terrain and never change stored generator output (decision D23).
